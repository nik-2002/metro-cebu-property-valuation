## Macro at a glance

- **Growth:** GDP grew **5.5% YoY in Q2 2025**; q/q growth **1.5%**. Agri rebounded (+7%), Services strong (+6.9%). ([Philippine Statistics Authority](https://psa.gov.ph/system/files/nap/Q2%202025%20NAP%20Highlights.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Q2 2025 NAP Highlights.pdf"))
- **Inflation:** **1.7%** in **Sept 2025** (up from 1.5% in Aug), still very low. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/philippine-annual-inflation-17-september-2025-10-07/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Philippine annual inflation at 1.7% in September"))    
- **Policy rate:** BSP **cut 25 bps to 4.75%** on **Oct 9, 2025** (ongoing easing cycle). ([Bureau of the Treasury](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/SitePages/PriceStability/MonetaryPolicyDecision.aspx?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Monetary Policy Decisions"))
- **Labor market:** **Unemployment 3.9%** (Aug 2025), underemployment down to **10.7%**. ([Philippine Statistics Authority](https://psa.gov.ph/statistics/labor-force-survey/press-release/node/1684080941?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Highlights of the August 2025 Labor Force Survey"))
- **FX context:** USD/PHP reference near **58.15** mid-Oct (useful for costed imports/materials). ([Bureau of the Treasury](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/sitepages/statistics/exchangerate.aspx?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas Statistics - Exchange Rate")) 
- **FDI/Investment:** **FDI net inflows $1.3B** (Jul 2025), **–7.5% YoY** but the **highest in a year**; H1 2025 weaker vs 2024. ([Bureau of the Treasury](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/SitePages/MediaAndResearch/MediaDisp.aspx?ItemId=7707&MType=MediaReleases&utm_source=chatgpt.com "FDI records US$1.3B net inflows in July 2025"))
- **Remittances:** Cash remittances **+3.2% YoY in Aug; +3.1% YTD (Jan–Aug)**—a steady tailwind for housing. ([Bureau of the Treasury](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/SitePages/MediaAndResearch/MediaDisp.aspx?ItemId=7713&MType=MediaReleases&utm_source=chatgpt.com "Cash remittances reach US$3.0B in August"))    

**Implication:** Easing inflation + rate cuts improve financing conditions; jobs remain solid; FDI is mixed (cautious external climate). Net effect is supportive for domestic consumption and residential demand, with selective pressure on commercial linked to global headwinds.

## Infrastructure (Build Better More + PPP)

- **Flagship program:** **~186–207 IFPs** under _Build Better More_ (BBM), **~PHP 9.6T / US$168–177B** across transport, water, health, and digital; intent to keep infra at **5–6% of GDP**. ([Bureau of the Treasury](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Pages/IRG/irg-files/Public-Private%20Partnerships%20in%20the%20Philippines%E2%80%99%20Infrastructure%20Flagship%20Projects%20%28April%202025%29.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "April 2025"))
    
- **PPP pipeline:** PPP Center indicates **expanding pipeline (~230 projects)**; several new projects added mid-2025 (transport, digital, tourism). ([Philippine PPP Center](https://ppp.gov.ph/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "PPP Center | The Official Site Public-Private Partnership ..."))
    
- **Cebu examples (trend signals):** **Cebu BRT** Phase 1 pilot slated 2025; implementation timeline affirmed despite leadership shifts. **Metro Cebu Expressway** still in prep (TOR revision). ([ABS-CBN](https://www.abs-cbn.com/news/business/2025/7/23/cebu-bus-rapid-transit-to-do-pilot-run-in-september-1648?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Cebu Bus Rapid Transit to do pilot run in September"))
    

**Implication:** National infra + PPP momentum typically **lifts corridor land values**, compresses travel times, and **supports suburban/edge-city development**—especially along mass transit and expressways.
