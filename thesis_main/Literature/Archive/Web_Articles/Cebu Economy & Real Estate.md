## What this says about Cebu’s **internal system** (economy → real estate)

**Economy & sectors**

- **Growth:** Cebu Province **+7.3% (2024)**; Region 7 also **+7.3%** and among the fastest in PH. Services (IT-BPM, tourism, trade) and Industry (manufacturing, construction) are main engines. [Philippine Statistics Authority+1](https://rsso07.psa.gov.ph/content/cebus-economy-grows-73-percent-2024?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **Tourism/air hub:** **MCIA 11.32M** passengers in 2024; **new intl. routes in 2025** support visitor & business travel—positive for hospitality, retail, and logistics. [Philstar+1](https://www.philstar.com/business/2025/01/28/2417292/cebu-airport-traffic-exceeds-11-million-passengers?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **IT-BPM:** Cebu is a **top GBS/BPO node** (≈**15%** of national FTEs), sustaining office demand in **Cebu IT Park** and nearby districts. [Bureau of the Treasury](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Pages/IRG/irg-files/DOF_Keynote_PEB%20Cebu.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **Manufacturing/PEZA:** Moves to **expand MEZ1** and build an **Aerotropolis ecozone** near MCIA point to continued industrial appetite. [SunStar Publishing Inc.](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/cebu/aetropolis-mez-1-expansion-in-cebu-eyed?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

**Infrastructure (value catalysts)**

- **CBRT (BRT):** Pilot operations **Nov 5, 2025** for Package 1; transit corridors typically **lift land values** and **densify** along stations (e.g., Capitol–IT Park–SRP). [Cebu Daily News](https://cebudailynews.inquirer.net/664481/cebu-brt-package-1-route-to-run-on-november?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **Metro Cebu Expressway (MCE):** **PPP-led** push to accelerate; once delivered, it **cuts north–south travel time**, unlocking hillside/backbone corridors. [Department of Public Works and Highways+1](https://www.dpwh.gov.ph/dpwh/PPP/projs/metro_cebu?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **MCIA & air connectivity:** Route expansions bolster **Mactan/Lapu-Lapu** as tourism–MICE hubs; supports **resort, STR, and retail** ecosystems. [Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/the-freeman/cebu-news/2025/09/26/2475546/mcia-unveils-new-routes-festival-flights-launch?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **CCLEX:** Operational third bridge improving Cebu City–Mactan accessibility; ongoing policy/toll/connection updates keep it central to **airport–city flows**. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cebu%E2%80%93Cordova_Link_Expressway?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

**Market snapshot**

- **Residential:** Pipeline is **large** (forecast ~**8.3k units/year** 2025–2028; **~102k** stock by 2028), so **absorption vs. deliveries** is the key watch. Suburban shifts to **Mandaue/Lapu-Lapu** continue as infra improves. [Philstar](https://www.philstar.com/the-freeman/cebu-business/2025/09/08/2471324/cebus-residential-boom-condo-supply-hit-102000-units-2028?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **Office:** **Stabilization** with **110k sqm** new supply in 2025, led by **Cebu IT Park**; expansions and pre-leasing are returning, aided by BPO/GBS hiring. [Colliers](https://www.colliers.com/en-ph/news/cebu-office-market-rebound-expansion-pre-leasing-flexible-workspaces?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **Industrial/Logistics:** **MEZ** expansion plans + airport-adjacent ecozone signal **capacity growth**; expect demand around **MEZ2**, **CLIP**, and port-served estates. [SunStar Publishing Inc.](https://www.sunstar.com.ph/cebu/aetropolis-mez-1-expansion-in-cebu-eyed?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **Tourism/Hospitality:** Passenger growth + new flights = **tailwind** for resorts/condo-hotels/retail in **Mactan** and along coastal corridors. [Philstar+1](https://www.philstar.com/business/2025/01/28/2417292/cebu-airport-traffic-exceeds-11-million-passengers?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
