## Abstract

Land is a key asset in wealth creation. It is one of the three sources or factors of production. Since 2008, Cebu City has experienced continued real estate boom and urban growth. Growth begets a myriad of problems such as traffic congestion, housing backlog, and unemployment. Land values are possibly the most fundamental factor in urban development planning. Understanding the determinants of land value is a basic requirement in policy making and in addressing the problems. This study examines 31 determinants of land value such as accessibility to transportation, neighborhood, distance to the city center, neighborhood, and others. The data set used was a result of a survey conducted with 52 real estate practitioners, valuers, assessors randomly selected, using a 5-point Likert scale questionnaire as a tool. In analyzing the data set, the research uses factor analysis, principal component analysis (PCA), and multiple regression with SPSS. In arriving at the ranking of the factors in land values, the study assigned as dependent variable land value data obtained from the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) zonal valuation. Based on the analysis, the priority and ranking can be categorized into:
- **Mobility**: Accessibility to public transportation
- **Livability**: Open space and parks, neighborhood, recreational facilities, environmental quality
- **Economic**: Accessibility to employment, rental income
- **Government Regulation and Taxation**: Zoning and government assessment and charges
- **Ownership**

The findings could be helpful in urban planning policy and decision-making processes. In particular, urban planners, investors, landowners, brokers, and valuers could use this model to evaluate the current situation and help in determining residential land prices.

## Introduction

Land is a key asset in wealth creation and one of the three sources or factors of production. Cebu City has experienced continued real estate growth since 2008, creating issues like traffic congestion, housing backlogs, and unemployment. Understanding the determinants of land values is essential for urban development planning. Real estate values directly impact location decisions and future urban developments. 

This study examines the determinants of residential land values in Cebu City, aiming to identify the variables that significantly affect land values. The findings contribute to studies in urban design, city planning, and real estate. The government can use the study's results to understand property prices, assessments, and the economic impact of land-use policies.

Cebu City is the capital of Cebu province and a major economic center in the central and southern Philippines, with a population of 922,611 (as of the 2015 census) and a population density of 2,900 per square kilometer. 

---

## Method

### Participants

The study focuses on residential land values in Cebu City. Residential properties, both owner-occupied and investment, are the most active market segments in Cebu City. Data was derived from primary and secondary sources, including a survey of 52 real estate professionals and secondary data from the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) zonal valuation and land-use maps.

### Materials and Procedure

31 major determinants of land values were identified, including factors like accessibility to employment, security, environmental quality, proximity to amenities, infrastructure availability, and zoning regulations. The data was analyzed using SPSS with Factor Analysis (Principal Component Analysis) and multiple regression to identify key contributing factors.

---

## Data Analysis and Discussion

### Factor Analysis

Factor analysis reduced the 31 variables to **11 significant factors**, explaining 82.65% of the variance. The factors were categorized into groups such as mobility, livability, economic factors, government regulations, and ownership.

### Principal Component Analysis

Eleven factors were extracted after applying PCA. The high-loading components included:
- **Component 1**: Open spaces and parks
- **Component 2**: Zoning regulations
- **Component 3**: Government assessments and charges
- **Component 4**: Neighborhood
- **Component 5**: Distance to city center
- **Component 6**: Rental income
- **Component 7**: Recreational facilities
- **Component 8**: Accessibility to public transportation
- **Component 9**: Accessibility to employment
- **Component 10**: Environmental quality
- **Component 11**: Ownership

---

## Findings

The study identified 11 key determinants affecting land values in Cebu City. These include economic, livability, location, and legal factors. Factors like accessibility to public transportation, recreational facilities, open spaces, parks, environmental quality, and ownership were most significant.

The factors were ranked based on their contribution to residential land values:
1. Accessibility to public transportation
2. Recreational facilities
3. Open spaces and parks
4. Environmental quality
5. Level of ownership
6. Government assessment and charges
7. Neighborhood
8. Rental income
9. Distance to city center
10. Zoning regulation
11. Accessibility to employment

---

## Conclusion

This study provides a ranking of the various factors that influence residential land values in Cebu City. The findings emphasize the need for policy makers and urban planners to consider these factors in decision-making to ensure optimal land use. These determinants will help guide real estate valuers, developers, and investors in evaluating residential land values and formulating better urban planning strategies.
