## Is there now an established standard for Real Estate prices in the Philippines?
### 🇵🇭 **Philippines: Established System for Real Estate Prices**
Yes, the Philippines now has an established system for tracking real estate prices. The Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP) introduced the **[[Residential Property Price Index]] (RPPI)** in 2016, which measures changes in residential property prices over time. In <u>Q1 2025, the BSP adopted the RPPI as its official house price index</u>, replacing the former **Residential Real Estate Price Index** (RREPI). This index is based on data from actual loans granted to acquire residential properties and is among the key indicators monitored by the BSP to assess the country's real estate and credit market conditions [Bureau of the Treasury+1](https://www.bsp.gov.ph/Pages/MediaAndResearch/PublicationsAndReports/regular_RREPI.aspx?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

The RPPI adheres to global best practices and serves as a more reliable gauge of housing price trends compared to the earlier index. It is calculated quarterly and is publicly available on the BSP's website.

### 🌍 **International Standards for Real Estate Price Indices**

Yes, there are now internationally recognized standards for compiling residential property price indices (RPPIs). The **Eurostat Handbook on Residential Property Price Indices** provides comprehensive guidelines agreed at the international level for the compilation of RPPIs. This handbook explains in depth the methods and best practices used to calculate an RPPI and examines the underlying economic and statistical concepts guiding the compilation of the indices [data.bis.org](https://data.bis.org/topics/RPP?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

Additionally, the **International Valuation Standards Council (IVSC)** develops international technical and ethical standards for valuations on which investors and others rely. The IVSC's standards aim to increase the confidence and trust of users of valuation services by establishing transparent and consistent valuation practices [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Valuation_Standards_Council?utm_source=chatgpt.com).
