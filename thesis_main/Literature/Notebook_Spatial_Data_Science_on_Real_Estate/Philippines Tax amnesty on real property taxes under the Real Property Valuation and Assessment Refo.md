Real Estate Tax

Tax Amnesty

In brief

RA No. 12001

Contents

Background

The tax amnesty does not apply to the following:

Delinquent real properties that have been disposed of at a public auction to satisfy the real property tax delinquencies.

Real properties with tax delinquencies that are being paid pursuant to a compromise agreement.
