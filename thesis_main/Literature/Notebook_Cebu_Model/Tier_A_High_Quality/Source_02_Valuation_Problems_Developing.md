## 1. Key Quantitative Findings
- **Sample Metrics**:
    - **Population**: 427 registered valuers in Kenya (Census approach).
    - **Response**: 132 valid returns (**31% response rate**).
    - **Experience**: 55.3% of respondents had >10 years experience.
- **Problem Ranking (Friedman ANOVA)**:
    - **1. Limited & Unreliable Information**: **Mean Rank 2.91** (Highest Impact).
    - **2. Corruption**: Mean Rank 2.43.
    - **3. Poor Land Info Management**: Mean Rank 2.34.
    - **4. Valuer Misconduct**: Mean Rank 2.32 (Lowest Impact).
- **Statistical Significance**: Differences were significant at **p < 0.001**.
- **Impact Scores**:
    - **90.2%** agreed "Information problems" cause inaccuracies.
    - **40.9%** rated it as having a "Very Strong Negative Effect".

## 3. Methodology
- **Research Design**: Cross-sectional survey of all registered valuers in Kenya.
- **Analysis**: Friedman ANOVA test to rank the severity of valuation challenges.
- **Constructs**: Measured perception of 4 distinct problem categories: Information, Corruption, Land Management, Misconduct.
