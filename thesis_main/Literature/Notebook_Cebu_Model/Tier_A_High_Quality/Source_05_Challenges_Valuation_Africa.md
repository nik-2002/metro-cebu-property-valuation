## 1. Key Quantitative Findings
- **Systematic Review Metrics**:
    - **Scope**: Screened **75,050** records; Final sample **25** articles.
    - **Research Activity**: Peaked in 2019 (only 4 articles), indicating "fragmented research."
- **Macroeconomic Correlations (Pearson)**:
    - **Urbanization vs. Domestic Credit**: **-0.935** (Strong Negative). *Meaning: As cities grow, formal credit shrinks.*
    - **Urbanization vs. FDI**: **-0.36**. *Urban growth does not automatically attract foreign capital.*
    - **GDP vs. FDI**: **+0.43**.
- **Longitudinal Trends (2010-2022)**:
    - **Credit Crunch**: Domestic credit dropped from **50.73%** (2010) to **33.37%** (2022).
    - **Agriculture**: Constitutes **40%** of GDP but suffers "systematic undervaluation" due to informality.
