## 1. Key Quantitative Findings

### Valuation Error Magnitude
- **Lagos Error Range**: **+24.8%** to **+51.5%** (over-valuation)
- **Global Benchmark**: ±10% is considered acceptable
- **Implication**: Lagos valuations deviate 2.5x to 5x from international standards

## 3. Methodology
- **Research Design**: Quantitative Survey Research
- **Data Collection**:
  - Primary: Questionnaires
  - Secondary: Personal interviews with NIESV officials and 10 Valuation Lecturers from 3 institutions
- **Target Population**: 500 Registered Surveyors and Valuers in Lagos (NIESV Directory, 2009)
- **Sample Frame**: 300 valuers in Estate Surveying and Valuation firms
- **Response Rate**: **50%** (150 out of 300 questionnaires returned)
- **Statistical Analysis**: Descriptive statistics (frequency tables, percentages)
- **Questionnaire Content**:
  - Firm characteristics (specialization)
  - Sources of information used for valuation
  - Availability of market evidence
  - Willingness to subscribe to a National Valuation Evidence Data
  - Ranking of factors contributing to valuation inaccuracy

## 4. Limitations & Future Research
- **Limitation (Sample Size)**: Only 150 respondents analyzed (50% response rate)
- **Limitation (Methodology)**: Survey measures *perception* of inaccuracy, not *actual* inaccuracy. No comparison to transaction prices.
- **Limitation (Geography)**: Lagos-only; findings may not generalize to other Nigerian cities or countries
- **Future Research**:
  - Creation of a **National Valuation Evidence Data (NVED) bank**
  - Academic curriculum reform to include contemporary valuation techniques
  - Specialization in practice to improve expertise
