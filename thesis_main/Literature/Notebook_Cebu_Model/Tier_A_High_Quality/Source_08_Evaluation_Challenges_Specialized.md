## 1. Key Quantitative Findings
- **Survey Metrics**:
    - **Population**: 51 registered firms.
    - **Sample**: 45 firms.
    - **Response**: 38 utilized questionnaires (**84% Response Rate**).
    - **Demographics**: 53.8% Directors; 39% had 6-10 years experience.
- **Method Preference (Likert 1-5)**:
    - **1. Replacement Cost**: **Mean 4.45** (RII 0.8895). *Dominant method.*
    - **2. Investment Method**: Mean 3.92.
    - **3. Sales Comparison**: Mean 3.79 (Least applicable due to data).
- **Challenges Ranking (RII)**:
    - **1. Lack of Comparable Sales**: **RII 0.8842** (Mean 4.42).
    - **2. Lack of Database**: RII 0.8789.
    - **3. Client Secrecy**: RII 0.8579.
