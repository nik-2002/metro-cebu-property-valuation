## 1. IVS Structure Overview

The IVS 2025 is divided into **General Standards** (all valuations) and **Asset Standards** (specific asset classes).

### General Standards

| Chapter     | Title                       | Key Requirements                                                                                   |
| ----------- | --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **IVS 100** | Valuation Framework         | Valuer Principles (Integrity, Objectivity, Competence), Quality Control                            |
| **IVS 101** | Scope of Work               | Client agreement on terms, disclosure of asset, intended use, basis of value                       |
| **IVS 102** | Bases of Value              | Market Value, Market Rent, Equitable Value, Investment Value, Synergistic Value, Liquidation Value |
| **IVS 103** | Valuation Approaches        | Market, Income, and Cost approaches                                                                |
| **IVS 104** | Data and Inputs             | **(NEW)** Selection and use of data; transparency requirements                                     |
| **IVS 105** | Valuation Models            | **(NEW)** Model selection, testing, and AVM requirements                                           |
| **IVS 106** | Documentation and Reporting | Transparency and disclosure requirements                                                           |

## 2. Key Requirements for Thesis

### IVS 104: Data and Inputs (Critical for Your Model)
The new chapter mandates:
1. **Accuracy**: Data must be accurate
2. **Completeness**: No material gaps
3. **Timeliness**: Current and relevant
4. **Transparency**: **"Source of the data can be traced from their origin"** (Provenance requirement)

> **Quote**: The chapter aims to "maximise the use of relevant and observable data."

### IVS 105: Valuation Models and AVMs (Critical for Your Model)
- **Professional Judgement Mandate**: 
  > "No model without the valuer applying professional judgement... can produce an IVS-compliant valuation."
- **Testability**: "Valuation models used must be tested to ensure accuracy of the output."
- **Implication for Thesis**: Your Cebu Model can be IVS-compliant **IF** it includes a human valuer review layer. Pure automation = not IVS-compliant.

### IVS 106: Reporting Requirements (Section 30.06)
Minimum disclosure requirements:
- (a) Agreed scope of work
- (b) Assets/liabilities being valued
- (c) Identity of the valuer
- (d) Client and intended use
- (g) Valuation currency and date
- (i) Basis of value adopted
- (j) Valuation approach adopted
- (k) Valuation method/model applied
- (l) **"Sources and selection of significant data and the key inputs used"**
- (m) **"Significant environmental, social and governance factors used and considered"**
- (n) Significant assumptions/limiting conditions
- (p) Conclusion of value and rationale
- (q) IVS compliance statement

## 3. New Changes from Previous Version
- **IVS 104 (Data and Inputs)**: Entirely new chapter addressing data quality and provenance
- **IVS 105 (Valuation Models)**: Entirely new chapter explicitly addressing AVMs and algorithmic models
- **ESG Integration**: New requirement to disclose ESG factors considered

## 4. ESG and Sustainability Requirements
IVS 2025 mandates consideration of ESG factors:
- **Environmental**: Climate change risks, biodiversity, waste management, energy efficiency
- **Social**: Human rights, working conditions, diversity, community relations
- **Governance**: Anti-bribery/corruption, board diversity, executive remuneration

> **Quote**: These factors should be considered "to the extent that they are measurable and would be considered reasonable by the valuer applying professional judgement."

## 5. Thesis Utility: "The Cebu Model"
*Relevance to Data-Driven Real Estate Valuation:*
- **Application**: **Regulatory Compliance**. Your model must satisfy:
  - IVS 104: Data provenance (use Explainable AI / SHAP values to trace inputs)
  - IVS 105: Professional judgement layer (model output reviewed by human valuer)
  - IVS 106: Transparent reporting (document all data sources and assumptions)
- **Relevance**: You can claim your model is "IVS-aligned" if you include a valuer review step. This makes it more credible to banks, regulators, and industry.
- **Limitation to Acknowledge**: Pure AVM output without valuer review is **NOT** IVS-compliant by design.
- **Chapter Reference**: Chapter 2 (Theoretical Framework—Global Standards), Chapter 3 (Methodology—Compliance Considerations)
