# **Summary of Related Literature**

## **Philippine Context & Key Studies**

| **Title / Author**                                                                              | **Objective**                                                                                                    | **Methodology**                                                                                                                                                                                             | **Results**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | **Significance to the Research**                                                                                                                                                                    |
| :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Determinants of Land Values in Cebu City**<br>(Agosto, 2017)                                  | To identify the significant factors influencing land values in Cebu City and their relative importance.          | **1.) Design:** Quantitative Survey of 133 Real Estate Appraisers and Brokers.<br>**2.) Analysis:** Factor Analysis (PCA) to reduce 25 variables into key components.                                       | **1.)** Identified 4 main factors explaining 68% of variance: Accessibility, Neighborhood/Environmental, Site/Physical, and Economic/Legal.<br>**2.)** "Accessibility" (proximity to main roads) was the dominant driver.                                    | **1.)** Provides the "Feature Wishlist": Distance to CBD, Road width, and Flood risk.<br>**2.)** Validates that meaningful pricing models in Cebu _must_ account for road network accessibility.    |
| **Real Estate Price Index: A Model for the Philippines**<br>(Domingo & Fulleros, 2005)          | To propose a framework for a national Real Estate Price Index (REPI) to address undervaluation and lack of data. | **1.) Data:** Administrative records (LRA Land Titles, BIR Tax Declarations).<br>**2.) Index:** Laspeyres Index methodology (Base Year 2000), pilot tested in select cities.                                | **1.)** Confirmed that Zonal Values are static and lag behind market prices by several years.<br>**2.)** Administrative data suffers from "undervaluation" due to tax avoidance, requiring cleaning/filtering.                                               | **1.)** Justifies the use of "Web Scraping" (BDO/Lamudi) to get _true_ market ask prices vs. reliable but low tax values.<br>**2.)** Establishes the "Valuation Gap" concept.                       |
| **Machine Learning vs. Hedonic Pricing (Central Pangasinan)**<br>(Viray, 2023)                  | To compare the predictive accuracy of traditional regression against machine learning models.                    | **1.) Data:** 2,300+ transactions + Zonal Values + Construction Cost Index.<br>**2.) Models:** OLS Linear Regression vs. Random Forest Regressor.<br>**3.) Metrics:** RMSE and MAE.                         | **1.)** Random Forest outperformed Linear Regression (lower RMSE).<br>**2.)** Non-linear models better captured the varying influence of location.<br>**3.)** Zonal values were a strong predictor but improved by 15-20% when combined with other features. | **1.)** Core precedent: We will replicate this comparison (Linear vs. RF/XGBoost).<br>**2.)** Validates the "Hybrid" approach: Official Data (Zonal) + Market Data (Listings).                      |
| **Enhancing Valuation with Government Indicators**<br>(Ramolete et al., 2023)                   | To test if adding government administrative data (services, boundaries) improves automated valuation models.     | **1.) Data:** Property listings augmented with distance to 12 facility types (Schools, Hospitals).<br>**2.) Models:** Gradient Boosting (XGBoost/LightGBM).<br>**3.) Validation:** K-Fold Cross Validation. | **1.)** Adding government features reduced Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) to ~21%.<br>**2.)** XGBoost provided the best balance of training speed and accuracy.<br>**3.)** Spatial features (proximity) remain critical.                              | **1.)** Validates the "Feature Engineering" plan (calculating distance to amenities).<br>**2.)** Confirms XGBoost as a state-of-the-art candidate for the thesis model.                             |
| **Metro Cebu: A Metropolitan Area in Need of Coordinative Body**<br>(Mercado et al., PIDS 2004) | To analyze the governance and spatial integration issues of Metro Cebu.                                          | **1.) Design:** Institutional/Policy Analysis.<br>**2.) Focus:** Urban planning synchronization across the 7 cities and 6 municipalities of Metro Cebu.                                                     | **1.)** Metro Cebu's fragmentation leads to uneven development (e.g., traffic bottlenecks affecting value).<br>**2.)** Lack of central coordination affects infrastructure planning.                                                                         | **1.)** Context for "Spatial Heterogeneity": Why values might jump abruptly across city boundaries (e.g., Cebu City vs. Mandaue).<br>**2.)** Supports treating "City/LGU" as a categorical feature. |

## **International & Methodological References**

| **Title / Author**                                                                    | **Objective**                                                                                 | **Methodology**                                                                                                                                                                 | **Results**                                                                                                                                                                                                           | **Significance to the Research**                                                                                                                                                           |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Explainable Machine Learning for Real Estate Valuation**<br>(Hu et al., arXiv 2024) | Systematic review of Explainable AI (XAI) in property tech to build user trust.               | **1.) Review:** Systematic analysis of 50+ recent papers (2018-2023).<br>**2.) Focus:** SHAP (Shapley Values), LIME, and Feature Importance methods.                            | **1.)** "Black box" models (RF/XGB) have high accuracy but low trust from stakeholders.<br>**2.)** SHAP is the "gold standard" for explaining individual predictions (e.g., "This house is +1M because of the pool"). | **1.)** Justifies the "Analysis" phase of Chapter 3: We _must_ generate Feature Importance plots to explain the model to CPRE (the client).                                                |
| **Exploring Spatial Segmentation from Online Listings**<br>(Sousa et al., 2024)       | To identify housing submarkets using only online listing data (no census data).               | **1.) Data:** Large-scale scraping of online real estate portals.<br>**2.) Method:** Community Detection/Network Analysis on price distributions.                               | **1.)** Online listings effectively capture market segments (luxury vs. mass market) even without official transaction data.<br>**2.)** Price clusters often defy official administrative boundaries.                 | **1.)** Validates "Online Listings" (BDO/Lamudi) as a viable dataset for market segmentation.<br>**2.)** Suggests clustering properties by price similarity.                               |
| **Interpretable Machine Learning**<br>(Molnar, 2022)                                  | Reference book on making black-box ML models understandable.                                  | **1.) Frameworks:** Partial Dependence Plots (PDP), Individual Conditional Expectation (ICE).<br>**2.) Theory:** Trade-off between interpretability and predictive performance. | **1.)** Establishes that intrinsic interpretability (Linear) < post-hoc interpretability (SHAP).<br>**2.)** Tree ensembles require post-hoc analysis.                                                                 | **1.)** The "User Manual" for interpreting the thesis's Random Forest results.<br>**2.)** We will use PDPs to show non-linear effects (e.g., price plateaus after a certain lot size).     |
| **Deep Learning for Real Estate Valuation: A Survey**<br>(Wang & Li, arXiv 2020)      | To review the application of Deep Learning (DL) in property valuation vs traditional methods. | **1.) Review:** Comprehensive survey of Neural Networks (CNN, RNN) in Real Estate.<br>**2.) Comparison:** DL (Image/Text) vs Hedonic vs Tree Ensembles (Structured).            | **1.)** DL excels with unstructured data (Property Images, Review Text).<br>**2.)** For pure tabular data (price, beds, size), Tree Ensembles (RF/XGB) are often equal/better and training is faster.                 | **1.)** Justifies _not_ using Deep Learning (unless we process images), confirming the choice of XGBoost/RF as the efficient choice for this tabular dataset.                              |
| **Macroeconomic Determinants of Housing Prices (Thailand)**<br>(Udomsap & Abid, 2020) | To examine the relationship between macro-factors (GDP, Interest Rate) and housing prices.    | **1.) Data:** Quarterly time-series data (2008-2019).<br>**2.) Method:** Vector Error Correction Model (VECM) for long-run relationships.                                       | **1.)** Interest rates and Construction costs are significant _negative_ drivers.<br>**2.)** GDP and Employment Rate are _positive_ drivers.                                                                          | **1.)** Supports the inclusion of "Macro-indicators" (Interest Rate/Inflation) if using time-series data.<br>**2.)** Suggests correcting for inflation when comparing 2024 prices to 2025. |
| **Property Price Prediction using Machine Learning**<br>(Gayathri & Thekkayil, 2025)  | To predict residential property prices using machine learning and a "Future Factors Database". | **1.) Data:** Historical property data (10 years) + Future Factors Database (infrastructure).<br>**2.) Models:** Ensemble of Random Forest and XGBoost.<br>**3.) Features:** Location, square footage, room counts, and future development plans. | **1.)** The ensemble model (RF + XGBoost) achieved 97.65% accuracy.<br>**2.)** Future infrastructure plans were significant predictors.<br>**3.)** Random Forest and XGBoost outperformed Linear Regression and ANN. | **1.)** Supports the use of Ensemble methods (RF/XGBoost) for high accuracy.<br>**2.)** Introduces the idea of "Future Factors" (infrastructure) which aligns with the thesis's focus on Cebu's development (CBRT/MCE). |
