- **The "Moderation" Myth**:
    - **Hypothesis**: Bank credit regulation should dampen bubble bursts.
    - **Finding**: Bank credit "**does not significantly moderate** the impact of housing bubbles." (i.e., Banks fail to stop the crash).
- **Lagged Effects**:
    - **Short-term**: Price hikes *temporarily* reduce risk (wealth effect).
