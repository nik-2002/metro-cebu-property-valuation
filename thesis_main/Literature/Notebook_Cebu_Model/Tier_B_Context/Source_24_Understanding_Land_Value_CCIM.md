## 1. Key Quantitative Findings
- **The Core Dynamic**:
    $$Land Value = Value_{AsComplete} - Cost_{Development}$$
- **Sensitivity**:
    - **Rising Costs**: A 1:1 deduction from Land Value.
    - **Time**: "Does not explicitly consider holding costs" (A limitation of the *simple* residual model).
- **Zoning impact**: "Differences in development density between sites may significantly deviate" (making Sales Comparison comparison "less credible" without adjustment).

## 2. Thesis Utility: "The Cebu Model"
*Relevance to Data-Driven Real Estate Valuation:*
- **Application**: **Feature Engineering**. You must include `Zoning_Density` (FAR) as a feature, because CCIM states that without it, comparisons are "less credible."
- **Relevance**: Supports the **"Transferred Value"** concept—where a business (e.g., a Mall) adds value to the land beyond just the dirt.
- **Theoretical Framework**: **Highest and Best Use (HBU)**.
