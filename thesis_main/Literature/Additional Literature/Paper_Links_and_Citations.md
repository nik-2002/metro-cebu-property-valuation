# Supplementary Literature for Chapter 2

> These are the 5 new papers discovered during the deep research phase to address the panel's feedback on GIS + ML in Southeast Asia, Philippine OSM validation, and custom value driver models.

## 1. Philippine Geospatial Amenity Accessibility

- **Title:** A framework for measuring geospatial amenity accessibility in the Philippines
- **Authors:** Alvarez, F. D., Madridejos, J. M., Sarmiento, J. A., Valdez, E., & Lecaros, L. L.
- **Year:** 2021
- **Journal:** *The International Archives of the Photogrammetry, Remote Sensing and Spatial Information Sciences*
- **DOI / Link:** [https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-archives-XLVI-4-W6-2021-19-2021](https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-archives-XLVI-4-W6-2021-19-2021)
- **PDF Downloaded**: Yes (`Alvarez_etal_2021.pdf`)
- *Note: This is "Project OHANA" which validates the use of OpenStreetMap (OSM) data for spatial analysis in the Philippines.*

## 2. Real Property Tax and LGU Income (Philippines)

- **Title:** Local government unit income and real property tax collection in the Philippines
- **Authors:** Otsuka, K., Manasan, R., & Piza, S.
- **Year:** 2023
- **Journal:** *Philippine Institute for Development Studies* (Discussion Paper)
- **Link:** [View on PIDS Repository / Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Local+government+unit+income+and+real+property+tax+collection+in+the+Philippines+Otsuka+2023)
- *Note: Proves that Zonal Values are outdated nationally (only 60% updated), justifying the Valuation Gap problem statement.*

## 3. Integrating Accessibility Indices into RF Models

- **Title:** Improving hedonic housing price models by integrating optimal accessibility indices into regression and random forest analyses
- **Authors:** Rey-Blanco, D., Zofío, J. L., & González-Arias, J.
- **Year:** 2024
- **Journal:** *Erasmus University Rotterdam Research Repository*
- **Link:** [View on Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Improving+hedonic+housing+price+models+by+integrating+optimal+accessibility+indices+into+regression+and+random+forest+analyses+Rey-Blanco)
- *Note: Provides the literature grounding for translating qualitative drivers (like Agosto's transport metric) into computational Accessibility Indices before feeding them to ML.*

- **Title:** Machine learning properties valuation: The role of non-historical and historical characteristics of heritage pre-war shophouses
- **Authors:** Samsudin, N. A., Husin, A., & Ali, H. M.
- **Year:** 2022
- **Journal:** *Estima*, 5(2), 24-41.
- **Link:** [View on Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Machine+learning+properties+valuation:+The+role+of+non-historical+and+historical+characteristics+of+heritage+pre-war+shophouses+Samsudin)
