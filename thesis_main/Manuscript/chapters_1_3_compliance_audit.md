# Chapters 1-3 Compliance Audit

Date: 2026-05-05

## Scope
This audit checks Chapters 1 to 3 against:
- BSDS Capstone Manuscript Format for AY2025-26
- Capstone Project Writing Guide
- APA 7th edition tense and structure reminders in the BSDS writing guide

## Overall Status
- Chapter 1: partially compliant
- Chapter 2: mostly compliant
- Chapter 3: partially compliant

## Chapter 1 Findings
### What already aligns
- Includes background, statement of the problem, research questions, significance, and scope and limitations.
- Uses mixed tense in a way that is broadly consistent with the guide: present for general background, present perfect or past for cited work, and past where needed.
- Establishes the Cebu-specific problem and the decision-support orientation clearly.

### What is still missing or weak
- No explicit statement of aim and objectives as its own subsection.
- No explicit definition of key terms section.
- No organization of the report section explaining the chapter flow.
- Minor prose issues remain and should be cleaned in a later writing pass, for example "property pricing system" and the typo "ony 60\%".

## Chapter 2 Findings
### What already aligns
- Covers theoretical foundations, prior studies, Cebu-specific literature, research gap, and a bridge to methodology.
- Uses tense mostly in line with the guide: past for author findings, present perfect for research trends, and present for general claims.
- The chapter closes with a clear research gap and transition.

### What is still missing or weak
- The writing guide recommends a comparison summary table; this is optional, so this is not a compliance defect, but the chapter may still benefit from one later.
- The title says "Review of Related Literature" rather than the guide's "Review of Related Works"; this is acceptable if the program allows that phrasing, but it is worth confirming.
- Section numbering should be checked later for duplicate numbering in the rendered PDF because the synthesis section currently uses 2.8 after an earlier 2.8 heading.

## Chapter 3 Findings
### What already aligns
- Proposal tense was corrected to final-paper tense in the methodology.
- Includes research design, tools, technologies, data sources, selected algorithms, and evaluation metrics.
- The chapter is operational enough to support replication at a high level.

### What is still missing or weak
- The writing guide asks for the framework or process model adopted and a justification for choosing it. Chapter 3 currently does not explicitly frame the workflow under CRISP-DM.
- Ethical considerations are not currently stated.
- Some section content still reflects an older manuscript state and should later be cross-checked against the latest ABT and model results before final defense writing.
- The data understanding, preparation, modeling, and evaluation details that the guide expects in Chapters 4 to 7 should not remain overloaded into Chapter 3 once those chapters are fully drafted.
