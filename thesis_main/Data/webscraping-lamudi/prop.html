Description
AMITA - Mid Two Storey Townhouse Other Key Features: porch ventanillas Amoa: A Vibrant Development in Compostela, Cebu About Amoa Amoa is a thoughtfully designed residential development in Compostela, Cebu, offering homes that blend Cebuano heritage with modern minimalist aesthetics. With seven enclaves, Amoa fosters a friendly neighborhood environment with homes designed to maximize natural light and ventilation, providing residents with a peaceful, refreshing lifestyle. Features and Amenities With homes designed to capture natural light and breezes, Amoa provides an energy-efficient lifestyle. Residents enjoy access to spacious pocket parks, playgrounds, and edible gardens that enhance community life. Amoa homes feature larger windows, a signature porch design, and interior spaces inspired by Cebuano architecture, creating a comfortable living environment. House Finishes Roofing: Pre-painted long-span roofing with insulation Flooring: Ceramic tiles, vinyl tiles, and gravel wash-out finishes Windows: Aluminum windows with Analok frames Doors: Painted metal doors (main); wood and PVC doors (interior) Ceiling: Painted gypsum board and soffit finishes Why Amoa Is a Perfect Residential Option in Cebu Amoa provides the perfect setting for families, professionals, and retirees seeking a peaceful community that blends modern amenities with Cebuano heritage. With its strategic location, flexible financing schemes, and thoughtfully designed homes, Amoa offers an unparalleled lifestyle in Compostela, Cebu. Location Amoa is located in Compostela, Cebu, approximately 40 to 45 minutes from Cebu’s Business District. The development’s location provides easy access to major business hubs, schools, hospitals, and shopping centers, ensuring that residents are well-connected. Surrounded by rolling landscapes and ocean views, Amoa offers an exceptional lifestyle, balancing city conveniences with a serene countryside setting. How to Get to Amoa Amoa is easily accessible through two main routes: Via Pacific Mall-Cansaga Area: Proceed towards Liloan. Via Public Highway: Travel straight, then turn left approaching UV Compostela. Its strategic location ensures residents can commute efficiently while enjoying proximity to nearby towns and beaches. Financing Options Amoa offers several financing schemes to accommodate buyers. Bank financing is available via BPI, BDO, Metrobank, UnionBank, RCBC, and EastWest, ensuring multiple financing options. Buyers may also co-borrow with first-degree relatives, providing flexibility in financing.
