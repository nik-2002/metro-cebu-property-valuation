# Course Introduction

Possible research advisors:

- Sir Ken
- Sir Randy
- Sir Elmer
- Ms. Millie
- Sir Camilo
- Ms. Jenice

Research consultant - expert in a specific domain on which the research project solution is applied.

Research panelist - reviews the quality of the research project deliverables.

Meet with your adviser at least 5 times.

We will get an adviser after our third presentation.

The thesis doesn’t have to be deployed; it just needs to be applicable.

You will present before a panel twice:

- Project proposal presentation
    - Compilation of revised project concept, literature review, and methodology
    - 30 minutes maximum for presentation
    - 10 minutes for question and answer
- Final project presentation

We will have three presentations before the first project proposal presentation before a panel:

- Project Concept (10 slides):
    - Background
    - Statement of the Problem
    - Research Objective
    - Solution Requirements
    - Significance
    - Scope and Delimitations
    - Dataset Source and Description
    - Methodology
    - Expected Analytics Results
    - Ethical Considerations
- Literature Review Presentation (10 slides) - based on at least 3 scholarly papers related to the student’s research project with summary of methodology and findings.
- Methodology Presentation (10 slides): explanation of methods regarding data exploration and algorithms to be used; should also include a plan for the entire research project.

If there is a prescriptive analytics aspect, include trading simulations based on a given trading strategy.

A colloquium is scheduled in the middle of the semester for students to present updates of their research projects.

Final research paper chapters:

1. Introduction
2. Review of Related Works
3. Methodology
4. Data Understanding
5. Data Preparation
6. Modeling
7. Evaluation
8. Results and Discussion
9. Conclusions
10. Recommendations

The final research paper must be submitted 1 week before the final project presentation. The panel is the same as the initial project presentation.

APA Style:

- 1-in margins on all sides of the page
- Use a legible font.
- Line spacing: double-spacing for the entire paper
- Paragraph alignment: align paragraphs to the left margin. Leave the right margin ragged. Do not use full justification. Indent the first line of every paragraph of text 0.5 in.
- Page numbers: Use the page-numbering function of your word processing program in the top right corner of every page, including the title page or cover page, which is page 1. Student papers do not require a running head on any page.
- Title Page Setup (all centered):
    - Page number (page 1) on the top right corner
    - Paper title (Bold)
    - Author names
    - Affiliation
    - Course number and name
    - Instructor name
    - Due date
- Repeat title at the top of the first page
- Use italics sparingly, only on first time use of a term
